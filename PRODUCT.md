## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML5, CSS (Tailwind CDN with bespoke utilities), and vanilla JavaScript. Lightweight multi-page architecture with zero build step and zero runtime dependencies.

## Users

- **Primary:** Owners and collectors of classic, vintage, and historic automobiles (concours d'elegance standard) in Kraków, Myślenice, and Małopolska seeking master-level interior reconstruction, period-correct leather, and authentic artisanal craft.
- **Secondary:** Discerning owners of modern luxury, sports, or premium daily vehicles seeking bespoke leather upholstery upgrades, leather steering wheel re-trimming (smooth, perforated, alcantara), headliner restoration, or damaged leather repair.

## Product Purpose

Establish digital authority as the leading bespoke saddlery atelier and automotive upholstery workshop in Lesser Poland. Educate clients on artisanal techniques, showcase flagship restorations, and guide qualified prospective clients directly to phone consultation (`605 304 999`) and in-person workshop appointments in Krzyszkowice. Success is measured in qualified, high-margin bespoke projects and client trust rather than commodity volume.

## Positioning

Authentic master saddlery lineage originating in 1998 in a traditional family workshop, grounded in equestrian craft, horse-drawn carriage restoration, and antique furniture before expanding to automotive coachbuilding and interiors. Unlike standard modern upholstery shops, Marcin Nowak provides bespoke artisanal reconstruction with period-correct materials (such as Daimler-Benz archive pattern cloths and premium automotive-grade hides). Explicitly non-commodity: no automated checkout or price discount claims, no rush promises; estimates and consultations are handled personally.

## Product Principles

1. **Craft Over Speed:** Communicate true artisanal dedication; never promise hasty turnarounds. Quality and authenticity take precedence over rush jobs.
2. **Phone as Direct Conversion:** Every page prioritizes immediate tap-to-call access to Marcin Nowak (`605 304 999`) and clear operating hours.
3. **Heritage as the Key Differentiator:** Highlight the 1998 saddlery and carriage restoration roots that elevate this workshop above ordinary car upholstery franchises.
4. **Honest and Transparent Expectation Setting:** Provide educational context on materials, leather grades, and restoration complexity without misleading price guarantees.
